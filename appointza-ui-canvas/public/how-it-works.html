How Appointza works

One platform connects people who book with businesses that run appointments. Here is the full picture, in plain language.

The big picture

  • A. Customers find a business (or open its booking link), pick a service or event, choose a time, confirm — and see everything in their account.
  • B. Businesses add locations, services, hours, and staff, then share a booking link. New requests appear on their dashboard; they can manage calendars, payments, and events in one place.
For customers

How booking works (service appointment)

Typical path when you book something with a time slot (salon, clinic, class, etc.).

Open app or link → Sign in if asked → Choose service & time → Pay / confirm → Done ✓
1

Find the business

Either use Explore to search organizations and services, or open the booking link the business sent you (WhatsApp, Instagram, website). No link? Explore helps you discover who is on Appointza.

You see real businesses, locations, and what they offer.

2

Pick services, staff & slot

Select what you need, optionally your staff member, then a date and time. Available slots come from the business’s real opening hours and time off — so you only see times they can actually take you.

No back-and-forth calls for “are you free Tuesday?”

3

Confirm & pay

Enter your details. If the business turned on online payment, you can pay by card or UPI (via their configured gateway). Otherwise you confirm and pay as they arrange (e.g. cash at visit). Your booking appears under Appointments and on your Dashboard.

Push or email reminders depend on your app settings and what the business enables.

Events (workshops, tickets, registrations)

The same idea applies: browse public events, open one, fill seats and any extra questions the host added, pay if required, then track everything under My event bookings (including check-in QR when the venue uses it).

For businesses

How your side works

You set up once, share one link, then operate from a single dashboard.

Locations & hours → Services & events → Booking link → Manage bookings
1

Set up your business

Add locations (branches), working hours and breaks, services you sell, and optional events. Invite staff and choose what each person can see (calendar only vs full settings). This controls what customers can book online.

2

Publish your booking link

From Templates / booking page, generate your shareable link per location. Customers use it 24/7. You can still add walk-ins with on-spot registration or book for client from your CRM.

3

Run the day from one place

Dashboard: summaries and search by phone. Appointments: calendar, status, staff assignment, visit records. Event bookings: registrations, payments, check-in. Clients: history and timelines. Connect payments when you want online prepay.

Why it feels “simple”

👤
Customers

One account to see upcoming visits, past history, events, and (when offered) pay online — without calling during business hours for every slot.

🏢
Business owners & staff

Availability and rules live in the system, so the public schedule stays accurate and the team spends less time on coordination.
